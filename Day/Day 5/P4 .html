Viewport: visible area of a webpage.It is the rectangle region where the browser actually renders and show content. Anything outside the viewport is not visible until we scrol.

CSS Units: the measurment units used to specifyy sizes,spacing and other length related values

1.Absolute units: fixed value:px(pixels),cm.ptp(oints),pc(picas) 2.Related units: relative to something else: em(relates to parent element) | rem (root em) | %percentage | vw(viewportwidth) | vh(viewport height)|vmin: 1% of the smaller dimension of viewport|vmax :1% of the bigger dimension of viewport | zero value: margin:0
